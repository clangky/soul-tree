# Soul Tree — cosmology and simulation grammar

## The central image

The universe is imagined as a growing body made from the trajectories of souls.
A soul is neither a disposable particle nor a miniature copy of the whole. It
is a persistent point of choice whose path contributes to a form no participant
can create alone.

The tree is reaching, but not along an ordinary straight line. Its crown curves
through death into its own root. Seen from one aspect this circulation is an
ouroboros; seen outside linear time it is a mandala. Tree, serpent, and sacred
geometry are therefore projections of one object.

## Rules

### Incarnation

Each soul repeatedly moves through two phases:

1. **Embodied ascent:** emergence at the shared root, differentiation,
   branching choice, encounter with the threshold, and death at the crown.
2. **Return current:** a faster exterior passage that carries the soul from the
   crown back to the root without depicting that interval as another ordinary
   life.

### Choice and inheritance

A soul begins with a lineage. On each life it can continue that inherited path
or depart into another branch. Choice controls the probability and distance of
that departure. Neither setting is morally privileged: continuity creates
memory; divergence creates possibility.

### Awakening

Every soul has a different recognition threshold. Awakening is not escape from
the tree. It is the realization that the individual path and the larger body
are simultaneously real. Visually, an awakened soul brightens and begins
bending toward the common destination while retaining its own phase and speed.

The Send a Pulse control temporarily increases the recognition field across
many lives. It is a question posed to the simulation: what happens when an idea
arrives in many places at once?

### Resistance

Resistance preserves the experience of separation near the crown. It deflects
and delays souls that have not recognized the whole. As coherence rises, the
boundary thins and becomes permeable.

This force is intentionally not personified as evil. Without resistance there
is no pressure, contour, or collective labor—only effortless convergence. The
simulation treats it as a condition that can become a doorway.

### Coherence

Coherence is an aggregate measure derived from the awakened proportion, reduced
slightly by extreme divergence. When coherence exceeds the resistance-defined
threshold, the rings fade and the destination grows more luminous. No single
soul breaks the boundary alone.

## Visual language

| Element | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Colored lights | individual souls or statistically equivalent aggregates |
| Shared lower convergence | the root beneath remembered identity |
| Branch direction | inherited trajectory plus choice in the current life |
| Gold-white lights | recognition of the unified body |
| Exterior falling arcs | reincarnation return current |
| Crown rings | forces maintaining separation |
| Crown light | an unknown destination, not a predefined heaven |
| Twelvefold top projection | lineage becoming mandala outside linear time |

## Future experiments

- Local-neighbor awakening instead of the current global recognition field, so
  beliefs and realizations spread through encounters.
- Soul relationships that recur across multiple lives and pull paths together.
- Distinct eras with changing physical laws rather than only changing narration.
- Collective mistakes: awakened groups may coordinate around the wrong attractor.
- Multiple destinations competing or revealing themselves as one structure.
- An archaeological layer where old collective forms remain faintly visible.
- Spatial sound in which each lineage is a voice and coherence becomes harmony.
- A recording mode that exports slow camera orbits as film.

The most important constraint is preserved throughout: this remains an artistic
instrument for thinking, not software claiming to calculate spiritual truth.
